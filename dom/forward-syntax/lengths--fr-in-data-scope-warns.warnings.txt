[gofish:layout] spread(by: "quarter") on x mixes data-driven and flexible sizes: its spacing fr(0.25) is flexible, but rect(key: "Q1"), rect(key: "Q2"), rect(key: "Q3"), rect(key: "Q4") are sized by data. A flexible size is like a data type with no name, so this is like sizing two parts along x by two different data types: no one scale fits both. its spacing fr(0.25) is drawn its weight in data units wide. Give it a size of its own, or put the data-driven parts in a container with a size of its own.
