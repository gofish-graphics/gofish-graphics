# 🐟 GoFish Weekly Update (2026-10-22 → 2026-10-29)

## Highlights
This week GoFish got much better at labeling and layout: labels can now sit on points, lines and areas, chart sizes mean something clearer, and bubble treemaps are now possible. There was also work on units and some CI and tooling cleanup.

## What changed
**New charts and labels**
- Bubble treemaps are now `pack` plus `enclose`, with per-group padding and spacing in pack ([PR #1226](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1226)).
![Bubble treemap](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pr-assets/pr-1226/bubble-treemap.png)
- `.label()` now has a `Place` strategy family for points, lines and areas. Labels avoid each other and anything listed in `avoiding`, and they support a halo and a background ([PR #1205](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1205)).
![Line end labels](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pr-assets/pr-1205/line-end-labels.png)
- Labels inside shapes now pick white or dark text by APCA contrast against the fill. This fixes hard-to-read text on colors like Tableau green, and `mix()` parsing is fixed too ([PR #1212](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1212)).
![Label colors after the fix](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pr-assets/pr-1212/after.png)

**Layout and API**
- The size you pass to `render` is now the size of the whole graphic. The size you pass to `chart()` is the size of its plot, and axes, titles and legends draw in gutters around it. Nested charts keep their axes ([PR #1168](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1168)).
- Units are now a domain plus traits, with `CanAdd`. Time is a unit, and column records are supported ([PR #1200](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1200)).
![Stacked bars of monthly revenue](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pr-assets/pr-1200/summable-stack-v2.png)

**Tooling and CI**
- Visual baselines now use `snapshots/main` plus only the branch's own accepts, so `main`'s baselines no longer go stale ([PR #1218](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1218)).
- A new PR gate hook opens PRs as drafts and runs simplify and code-review before a PR is marked ready ([PR #1211](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1211)).
- The weekly summary for 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22 was added ([PR #1193](https://github.com/gofish-graphics/gofish-graphics/pull/1193)).
