Survey responses

    Distances are in px of the shapes' own size (56 px). The combined ranking is each model's mean distance over the widgets you set, and the count beside it says how many widgets the mean covers. An asterisk marks a model whose two measures never become equal in a widget; it has no position there, so that widget counts as missing for it, not as a large distance. A dagger marks a model whose measures are equal only where both are under a third of what it measures for a plain strip between two squares at the widget's own scale of gap (30 px for the shapes, Georgia's H to H gap for the letters, 24 px for the densities): there the model sees almost no gap on either side. On the two Georgia widgets, a dashed tick marks Georgia's own spacing (no kerning). The survey responses are the "Spacing by eye" results saved in results/: each person's position is an ink tick with a dot, on the other side of the shapes from the models, and the bold tick is their mean. Their ranking is each model's mean distance per person (over the widgets that person set), averaged over the people. The tables behind this are computed by widgetRun.ts from the same code as the renders.
