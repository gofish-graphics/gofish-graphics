Move the square, then the triangle, to where the gaps look even to you. Drag the shape or use the slider. Each model predicts the position where its own measure of the two compared gaps is equal; show the predictions when you are ready, and the ranking says which model is closest to you.

1. Square to triangles. The triangles are fixed 30 px apart.

2. A triangle between a circle and a square, which are fixed.

Closest to you, widget 1

  1. Move the square first.

Closest to you, widget 2

  1. Move the triangle first.

Both (mean distance)

  1. Set both.

Distances are in px of the shapes' own size (56 px). An asterisk marks a model whose two measures never become equal; its position is where they come closest. A dagger marks a model whose measures are equal only where both are under a third of what it measures for the 30 px strip between A and V: the fillet area on widget 2 never sees more than 15 px of gap next to the circle, so it cannot place the triangle. The tables behind this are computed by widgetRun.ts from the same code as the renders.
