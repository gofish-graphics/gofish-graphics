Spacing by eye, part 2

Each picture below is a row of shapes, and one of them moves. Drag it, or use the slider under it, until the gap the text names looks the same as the other one. There is no right answer. Go by eye, not by measuring, and don't think about it too long. Squinting can help. The Squint control at the top of the screen blurs the shapes, and its slider sets how much. The Zoom slider beside it makes the pictures larger or smaller.

When you are done, copy your results and send them to Josh.
