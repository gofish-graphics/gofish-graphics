Spacing by eye, part 2

Each picture below has one shape you can move. Drag it, or use the slider under it, until the spaces between the shapes look even to you. In most pictures, two squares at the top show a gap. Move the lower shape until its gap looks the same as that one. There is no right answer. Go by eye, not by measuring, and don't think about it too long. Squinting can help. The Squint control at the top of the screen blurs the shapes, and its slider sets how much. The Zoom slider beside it makes the pictures larger or smaller.

When you are done, copy your results and send them to Josh.
