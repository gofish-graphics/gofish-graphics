# LLM authoring benchmark v0: claude-opus-5, 2026-09-27

This is the first full run, at commit 6aefaa1e. The model was claude-opus-5 at
medium effort, with 2 samples per task and up to 3 turns per job. The GoFish arm
had `context/gofish.md` in its prompt, and the other arms had no docs. The docs
pack was not changed after any results were seen.

Notes for reading the results:

- Two samples per task is small. The confidence intervals below are wide, and
  most tasks sit at the ceiling in every arm.
- Both GoFish failures on `edit/stacked-to-grouped` are correct charts. They
  fail only the "keep size" part of the preserved score, because GoFish adds
  per-series labels beside each group by default and the chart grew from 622
  to 668 px wide. The tolerance is 3%. Without the size criterion, GoFish
  passes 36 of 38 jobs, not 34 of 38.
- The docs pack does not cover the mosaic pattern (`stack` with `size`),
  `repeat()`, or operator `.label()`. So the beyond-defaults group tests the
  pack as much as the library.

The generated report follows.
