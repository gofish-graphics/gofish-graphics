Enclose Outline Playground

Move the sliders to redraw the unified enclose outline live. It is the prototype's own code: links join the members, an exact closing by a disc of radius r fills the gaps (convexity picks r), keep-outs are cut out, and a Gaussian smoothing rounds the result. Above convexity 1 the hull blends toward the oval. The two switches under convexity try other meanings for it; turn on compare modes to see three side by side.
