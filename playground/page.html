Enclose Outline Playground

Move the sliders to redraw the prototype enclose outline live, with the prototype's own code. Blob: the members' join tree keeps connection of its links, which splits the members into pieces; each piece's padded members and links are filled to convexity (0 the spine, 1 the padded hull, 2 the oval) and smoothed. Fillet: the padded members, and links neck wide between pieces nearer than twice the radius, closed by a disc of radius (the padding unless set), so every inside corner is an arc. avoiding and clearance are options of enclose next to padding, and cut any shape.

The width of each link as a share of the padded members it joins: 1 is no neck.
▲ padding
The radius of every inside corner. By default it is the padding, so inside corners are as round as the outside ones. Members join where the closing disc cannot pass between them.
